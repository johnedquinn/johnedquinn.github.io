I'm John, a compiler and database enthusiast from Seattle, WA.

I'm currently working on PartiQL ([language website](https://partiql.org), [documentation](https://partiql.org/plk/1.0/index.html), [codebase](https://github.com/partiql/partiql-lang-kotlin)), an open-source query language developed by Amazon. It's a pretty cool language, and I do most of my work in Kotlin. If you're familiar with query languages, PartiQL is kind of like SQL, but it allows for querying heterogeneous and semi-structured data.

Outside of my time at PartiQL, I like to mess around with whatever is on my mind. Sometimes I'm working on some software project. Sometimes I'm out backpacking, skiing, or biking. Sometimes just building something in my garage. The things I do really depend on the season, especially with how rainy/snowy it gets in the Pacific Northwest.
