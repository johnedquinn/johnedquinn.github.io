I'm John, a compiler and database engineer based in Seattle, WA.

I work at Amazon on [PartiQL](https://partiql.org), an open-source query language for heterogeneous and semi-structured data. Most recently I've been building `PartiQLVM`, a register-based bytecode virtual machine in Rust, and previously led the [v1.0.0 release](https://github.com/partiql/partiql-lang-kotlin/releases/tag/v1.0.0) of the JVM implementation.

Outside of work I'm usually doing something seasonal — backpacking or biking when it's dry, skiing when it isn't, and building things in the garage year-round.
